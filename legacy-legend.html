Chronicle of Greatness

A Journey Across Generations

1924 — The Birth of an Icon

Moorkoth Vengakandy Shankaran, popularly known as Gemini Shankarettan, was born on June 13, 1924 in Kollassery, Thalassery to Raman Kavanassery and Moorkoth Vengakandy Kalyani.

Early Trapeze Career

Shankaran joined Boselion Circus in Kolkata as an agile trapeze and horizontal bar artiste, followed by celebrated performances with the Great Rayman Circus.

August 15, 1951 — Gemini is Born

On India’s Independence Day in Billimoria, Gujarat, Shankaran along with K. Sahadevan inaugurated the maiden show of Gemini Circus, marking the dawn of a legendary legacy.

1964 — World Stage & Moscow Festival

Late M.V. Shankaran led India's delegation to the World Circus Festival in Moscow, Sochi & Yalta. Praised by Alexander Voloshein and Soviet cosmonaut Valentina Tereshkova, the troupe was honoured with Indian diplomat visas.

Today & Beyond

Continuing as India’s foremost circus troupe featuring 50+ international artistes from Russia, Tanzania, and Ethiopia alongside seasoned domestic gymnasts.
